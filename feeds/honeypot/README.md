# Honeypot indicators

Machine-generated by a small public honeypot (T-Pot + custom decoys). Refreshed a
couple of times a day.

- `iocs.csv` — `kind,value,context,count,first_seen,last_seen`
- `iocs.stix.json` — STIX 2.1 bundle of the same indicators

**No IP addresses.** Scanner IPs are almost always compromised devices or shared
hosting: publishing them harms victims and goes stale within days. Only durable,
non-identifying indicators are here — payload hashes, crypto payout addresses, SSH
client fingerprints and injected public keys.

Consume what is useful, ignore the rest, and do not treat presence here as proof.
